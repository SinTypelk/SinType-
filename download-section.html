What is SinType?

SinType is a smart typing app for Windows that instantly converts the way you type English letters into beautiful Sinhala script, working everywhere on your computer.

Key Features

  • Type Sinhala Anywhere

    Works in Word, Photoshop, WhatsApp, Discord, browsers—any app on your PC. Just press F10 to turn Sinhala typing on or off.

  • Two Typing Modes

    Switch between two styles: Modern Unicode Sinhala or Legacy FM Abhaya fonts for design work.

  • Control Right From Your Phone

    Use your mobile phone as a remote touchpad and keyboard. Connect via a simple QR code scan on your home network.

  • Send Files From Mobile to PC

    Drag and drop files from your phone directly to your computer over your private home network—no cloud needed.

  • Make Your Own Typing Rules

    Customize how Singlish shortcuts map to Sinhala letters. Save your personal typing dictionary.

  • Everything Stays Private

    Your typing stays on your computer. Nothing is sent to the internet or stored in the cloud.

How to Install

  1. Download the Installer

    Click the download button above. Your browser will save the SinType installer file.

  2. Run the Installer

    Open the downloaded file and follow the setup wizard. The app will be installed to your Programs folder.

  3. Launch SinType

    After installation, find SinType in your Windows Start menu or desktop shortcut. Click to open the app.

  4. Activate Your License

    Open the License tab inside the app, enter your email and activation key (from sintype.lk/license), and click Activate.

How to Activate Your License

Every PC gets its own license key tied to that computer's hardware fingerprint. Here's how to activate:

  1. Get Your Activation Key

    Go to sintype.lk/license, sign in with your email, and copy your 30-day free key.

  2. Open the License Tab

    In the SinType app, click the License tab. Paste your email address and the activation key you just copied.

  3. Click Activate

    The app connects to our server to verify and activate your license. You'll see a confirmation message once it's active.

Note: Your license is linked to this specific computer using its hardware fingerprint. If you use SinType on another PC, you'll need a separate key.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need the internet to use SinType?

Internet is only needed to activate your license and check for app updates. Once activated, you can type Sinhala offline without any internet. The typing conversion happens entirely on your computer.

Can I use SinType on multiple computers?

Each PC needs its own license key because each computer has a unique hardware fingerprint. You can request a new key for another computer on the same email account at sintype.lk/license. This protects your license and keeps the app secure.

How do I uninstall SinType?

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed Apps, search for "SinType", and click Uninstall. Or you can use the uninstall button in the SinType app's Settings tab—it will remove the app cleanly. Your settings and custom typing rules are kept in your user folder just in case you want to reinstall later.
